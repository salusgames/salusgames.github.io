Challenge your mind and test your skills with **Hexa**, a minimalist puzzle game that will keep you hooked for hours. Use the different hex side types to get the beams to their destination. As you progress through the levels, the puzzles become increasingly complex, requiring you to think strategically and creatively in order to solve them.

## Features

- **33 handcrafted levels** with varying degrees of difficulty
- **Random level generator** for infinite replayability
- **Integrated level editor** — create and share custom levels via Steam Workshop
- **Four unique hex side types**: Link, Converter, Splitter, and Switch
- **Minimalist art style** and clean design
- **Steam Achievements** (4 total)
- **Available on** Windows, Mac, and Linux
