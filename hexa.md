Challenge your mind with **Hexa**, a minimalist puzzle game built around unique hexagonal tile mechanics. Rotate and place hexes to guide beams of light through increasingly complex puzzles. Easy to learn, tough to master.

**Key features:**
- **33 handcrafted levels** with escalating difficulty
- **Unlimited random levels** via the built-in level generator
- **Level editor** — create and share levels on Steam Workshop
- **4 Steam Achievements** to unlock
- **Simple controls** — pick up and play in seconds

Whether you're a seasoned puzzle fan or just looking for a quick brain teaser, Hexa delivers a satisfying challenge you'll come back to again and again.
