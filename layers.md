**Layers** is a puzzle platformer with a unique twist: every level is built from two parallel layers — White and Black. Switch objects between layers, avoid deadly traps, and guide every character safely to the exit door. One wrong move and it's game over.

**Key features:**
- **Unique dual-layer mechanic** — interact with two parallel versions of each level
- **10 Steam Achievements** to unlock
- **Level editor** — build and share custom levels via Steam Workshop
- **Tight, focused puzzles** — every level is a carefully crafted challenge
- **Family friendly** — suitable for all ages

If you love puzzle games that make you think differently, Layers delivers a fresh mechanic you won't find anywhere else.
